🕹️ CONTROLS & INFO

Rules of the Game

  • Devour glowing red energy cores to score points and grow longer.
  • Each eaten core increases the speed level, making movement faster.
  • Colliding with walls or eating your own tail results in an instant GAME OVER.
  • Entering a top 10 score prompts you to save your name to the local leaderboard.

Desktop Key Bindings

W or ▲ : Move Up

A or ◀ : Move Left

S or ▼ : Move Down

D or ▶ : Move Right

Space : Pause / Resume, or Restart at Game Over
